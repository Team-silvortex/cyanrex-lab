# Community Code of Conduct

cyanrex-lab welcomes learners, educators, operators, and contributors with
different backgrounds and levels of eBPF experience.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Assume good intent while discussing the observable behavior of code or docs.
- Make technical decisions based on teaching value, safety, evidence, and
  maintainability.
- Help newcomers reproduce problems without shaming missing knowledge.
- Respect privacy; sanitize logs, screenshots, and classroom data.
- Accept maintainers' moderation decisions and move disagreements back to the
  technical question.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Pressuring someone to reveal credentials, identity, employer, or student data.
- Using project infrastructure or examples to target systems without explicit
  authorization.
- Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope and Enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issue trackers,
project-run community spaces, and public interactions made on behalf of the
project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens
the community or its users. Moderation should be proportional, documented when
safe, and avoid exposing private information.

Report urgent abuse through GitHub's reporting tools. For project moderation,
contact a repository maintainer privately when a private channel is available.
Do not use a public issue if doing so would expose a target or confidential
information.
